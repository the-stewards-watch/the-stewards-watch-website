Services & Rates

All services are provided in-home within Putnam & Dutchess Counties. Your pets stay in their own environment — comfortable, familiar, and stress-free.

Drop-In Visit

Up to one hour in your home. Includes feeding, a walk, playtime, fresh water, a plant check, and mail collection.

$35 / visit
+$30 for each additional hour

Dog Walk

A dedicated 30-minute walk for your dog. Can be booked as a standalone service without a full drop-in visit.

$25 / walk

Overnight House Sitting

We stay at your home from approximately 7pm to 7am. Includes all pet care and home security — mail, lighting, and keeping your home looking occupied.

$105 / night

24-Hour In-Home Care

Full-day, full-night presence in your home. The most comprehensive option for pets that need continuous company or for longer trips.

$155 / day

Weekly Rate

Seven consecutive nights of overnight house sitting. Includes all pet care and home maintenance throughout your trip — the best value for extended travel.

$875 / week
7 overnight stays

Home & Plant Care

Regular check-ins to water plants, collect mail, adjust lighting, and keep an eye on the property. Available for households without pets.

$35 / visit

Add-On Additional Cost

Senior pet or special needs care +$10 / visit
Each additional pet +$10 / visit
