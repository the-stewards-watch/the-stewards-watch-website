About The Steward's Watch

Our Story

The Steward's Watch was founded right here in the Hudson Valley, rooted in the belief that your home deserves the same care and attention you'd give it yourself. Based in Kent/Carmel, NY, we serve homeowners across Putnam and Dutchess Counties who need someone they can truly trust while they're away — whether for a long weekend, a vacation, or an extended trip.

We started this business because we know how hard it can be to leave your home behind. Finding someone reliable, local, and genuinely invested in the well-being of your property shouldn't be a stressful experience. That's why we built a service centered entirely on trust.

Our Approach

Great house sitting starts with genuine attention to detail. Before every engagement, we take the time to understand your home's specific routines, your pets' habits, and your personal preferences. During your absence, we're not just checking boxes — we're truly watching over your home as if it were our own.

We provide regular updates and photo check-ins throughout your time away, so you can relax and be present wherever you are, knowing your home is in good hands.

Who We Are

The Steward's Watch is owner-operated by Matthew Bestard, a local to the Kent/Carmel area. Matthew brings a personal commitment to every client relationship and handles every engagement directly — no subcontractors, no strangers, no surprises. When you hire The Steward's Watch, you always know exactly who is in your home.

As a neighbor and community member in Putnam County, Matthew understands how important your home is to you, and takes that responsibility seriously.

Our Promise

When you hire The Steward's Watch, you're not getting a faceless service — you're getting a neighbor who takes your trust seriously. We are committed to open communication, careful attention, and leaving your home exactly as we found it.

Your peace of mind is our measure of success.
