About The Steward's Watch

Meet the Founder

The Steward's Watch was founded by Matthew Bestard, a software engineer who spent years building products for companies before deciding he wanted to build something of his own — something with a direct, visible benefit to real neighbors rather than another platform designed to extract and monetize user data. That desire to do something more tangible and personal is what eventually led him here, to this community, doing this work.

Matthew is the person who shows up at your home, every time. That's not a marketing line — it's the whole model.

Our Background

A software engineering career teaches you how to solve hard problems, build things from scratch, and think carefully about details. We wanted to take those skills somewhere different — away from building products for other companies, toward something that directly helps neighbors with something they need in their daily lives. We wanted to experiment, make decisions, and grow in the direction we chose, not in the direction someone else dictated.

How It Started

It started the way a lot of good ideas do — just doing something useful for family. When relatives needed to travel or get away for a few days, we started watching their homes and pets for them. We were already people they trusted, we were local, and we took it seriously. At some point it struck us that this was something we could build into a real business — that there were plenty of people in this area who needed exactly this kind of help and had a hard time finding someone they could actually rely on.

Our Experience

Our experience didn't start at full engagements. It built up gradually the way real experience does. A 30-minute check-in here, a few hours the next time, then an overnight stay, then watching a home for a full week while the owners were on vacation. Along the way we've handled everything that comes with it — feeding and walking dogs, collecting mail, making sure everything in the house is as it should be, and keeping owners updated throughout.

We've taken care of dogs across the size spectrum — large breeds like Great Danes, Pit Bulls, and Black Labs, as well as smaller dogs. We've also cared for dogs that came home from the vet after surgery, which requires extra patience, attentiveness, and a calm presence. Each situation is different, and we've learned to read what each animal and each household needs.

What You're Getting

When you hire The Steward's Watch, you're hiring us directly, every time. No subcontractors, no strangers, no surprises. We handle every engagement personally, the same way we did when we were doing this for family. We know how important it is to leave your home in good hands, and we don't take that lightly. That engineering background carries into every visit — close attention to detail and clear communication are things we bring to every home we watch over.

Your peace of mind is the whole point.
