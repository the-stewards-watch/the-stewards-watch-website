About The Steward's Watch

Where I'm Coming From

I spent years working as a software engineer. It's a career that teaches you a lot — how to solve hard problems, how to build things from scratch, how to think carefully about systems and details. But over time I found myself wanting to use those skills differently. I wanted to build something of my own, on my own terms, rather than spend my energy making a product for a company. I wanted to do something where I could see the benefit to real people directly — not another platform designed to extract and monetize user data, but something that actually helps neighbors with something they need in their daily lives. I wanted to experiment, make decisions, and grow in the way I chose to, not the way someone else told me to.

That desire to do something more tangible and personal is what eventually led me here.

How It Started

It started the way a lot of good ideas do — just doing something useful for family. When relatives needed to travel or get away for a few days, I started watching their homes and pets for them. I was already someone they trusted, I was local, and I took it seriously. At some point it struck me that this was something I could build into a real business — that there were plenty of people in this area who needed exactly this kind of help and had a hard time finding someone they could actually rely on.

Experience Built Over Time

My experience didn't start at full engagements. It built up gradually the way real experience does. A 30-minute check-in here, a few hours the next time, then an overnight stay, then watching a home for a full week while the owners were on vacation. Along the way I've handled everything that comes with it — feeding and walking dogs, collecting mail, making sure everything in the house is as it should be, and keeping owners updated throughout.

I've taken care of dogs across the size spectrum — large breeds like Great Danes, Pit Bulls, and Black Labs, as well as smaller dogs. I've also cared for dogs that came home from the vet after surgery, which requires extra patience, attentiveness, and a calm presence. Each situation is different, and I've learned to read what each animal and each household needs.

What You're Getting

When you hire The Steward's Watch, you're hiring me — directly, every time. No subcontractors, no strangers, no surprises. I handle every engagement personally, the same way I did when I was doing this for family. I know how important it is to leave your home in good hands, and I don't take that lightly. My background taught me to pay close attention to details and to communicate clearly — and I bring both of those things to every visit.

Your peace of mind is the whole point.

Want to talk about what you need? Reach out and let's figure it out together.
